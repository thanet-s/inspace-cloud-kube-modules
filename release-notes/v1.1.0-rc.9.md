## E2E edge capacity check ignores replaced NodeClaims

`v1.1.0-rc.9` builds on `v1.1.0-rc.8`.

- With the worker registry egress gate from `v1.1.0-rc.8`, an edge node on a
  bad floating IP never registers and Karpenter replaces it. The replaced
  NodeClaim stays listed while its VM, floating IP, and firewall relation are
  removed. The E2E edge capacity wait required exactly one NodeClaim, so the
  `v1.1.0-rc.8` E2E failed even though the replacement edge node was Ready.
  The wait now counts only NodeClaims that are not being deleted, like the
  general worker check.
- The `v1.1.0-rc.8` E2E runs also showed the new control-plane egress check
  catching two control planes on floating IPs without internet egress within
  five minutes, followed by the unattended destroy-and-retry.

## Upgrade notes

No product change from `v1.1.0-rc.8`.
