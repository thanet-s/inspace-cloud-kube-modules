## v1.1.1

### Storage

- The `csi.inspace.cloud` CSIDriver now sets `fsGroupPolicy: File`, so kubelet
  applies a Pod's `securityContext.fsGroup` to `inspace-rwo` volumes. Before,
  a new volume stayed owned by root and non-root workloads (Prometheus,
  Grafana, PostgreSQL, ClickHouse and others) could not write to it.
  Upgrading changes the existing CSIDriver in place; running Pods pick up the
  ownership when they restart.
